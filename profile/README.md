# ALSONTECH | Industrial 3D Vision Solutions

ALSONTECH specializes in industrial 3D vision technology, developing and providing 3D cameras and machine vision solutions for robotic automation and industrial applications.

Our solutions support a wide range of applications, including robotic picking, machine tending, depalletizing, palletizing, bin picking, and industrial inspection.

## Our Products

- Laser 3D Cameras
- Structured-Light 3D Cameras
- 3D Laser Profilers
- Industrial 3D Vision Systems

## Industrial Applications

ALSONTECH 3D vision technology helps robots perceive objects, identify positions, and perform accurate picking and handling tasks in complex industrial environments.
